The brand

Care the way Indian homes have always done it, checked the way science does.

Nanhe (नन्हे) is how Hindi speakers talk about little ones. It is the word a grandmother uses at the first oil massage.

The products use the ingredients those homes already trust, like coconut, almond, oat, neem, ghee and calendula. Each formula is clinically tested on infant skin before it goes on sale. Every pack lists everything inside it, and says what percentage of it comes from nature.

Natural first

Plant oils, butters and extracts lead every formula. We publish the naturally derived percentage on each pack.

Clinically tested

Each formula is tested on infant skin under a dermatologist's supervision before launch. The study details are printed on the back.

Nothing hidden

Full ingredient lists in plain words, with what each ingredient is there to do. We don't use vague claims like "chemical-free".

Logo

The sprouting h

The wordmark is a single rounded line, as soft as the products. A new leaf grows from the h. That leaf is the brand's signature, and it always stays leaf green, or pale sage on dark backgrounds.

Claims & icons

Every promise comes with its proof

The seal and icons follow one rule: anything we claim on the front is backed up on the back. That means the study, the sample size, the percentage, or the list of what's left out.

*The back of every pack explains the claim, for example: "Tested under dermatological supervision on [n] infants aged [x–y] months, [lab name], [year]." The numbers come from our test reports, not from marketing.

The line

Nine products, three colours, one family

Every pack is matte milk-white with the wordmark in the same place. Only the cap and the category text change colour, so the line looks calm on a shelf and each product is easy to find.

MoisturiseLotion in a 200 ml pump, massage oil in a clear bottle that shows the oil, and a rich cream in a 100 g jar.
Care & ProtectRash cream and sunscreen in 50 g tubes that stand on their caps, and a 25 g balm tin for the changing bag.
Everyday HygieneTalc-free powder in a sifter shaker, bottle wash in a 300 ml foam pump, and 72 wipes in a soft pack with a rigid lid.
